

.. _sphx_glr_examples_plasticity:

Plasticity
----------

Elastoplastic structures in small and finite strain, with Simcoon and native
constitutive laws (bending, simple shear, torsion), and heterogeneous
structures: a matrix with an inclusion, linear elastic or elastoplastic, with
three ways to assign a material to each element set.
