Custom weak forms
-----------------

Problems defined directly from a user weak form written with fedoo's
differential operators: Stokes and Navier-Stokes flows. The Poisson equation is
in the basic examples (:ref:`sphx_glr_examples_basic_poisson_eq.py`).
