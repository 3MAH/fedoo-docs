Homogenization
--------------

Effective properties of periodic and non-periodic microstructures, and
FE\ :sup:`2` multiscale analysis.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="A quarter of a plate with a hole (2D plane strain, very coarse quad4 mesh) is stretched along x. Its material is not given by a constitutive law but computed by FE\ 2: with fedoo.constitutivelaw.FE2, each macroscopic integration point carries a 3D finite element model of an octet-truss unit cell (``tet4`` mesh) made of an elastoplastic steel (simcoon EPICP). At every Newton iteration, the macroscopic strain is applied to each cell with periodic boundary conditions, and the cells return the mean stress and the homogenized tangent matrix.">
