Contact and interfaces

Contact between deformable bodies and self-contact, with the penalty method and with the Incremental Potential Contact (IPC) barrier, compared with analytical solutions (Hertz, Westergaard) where they exist. The folder also covers self-contact in 2D axisymmetric models (tube compression) and cohesive interfaces: Composite double-cantilever beam with a cohesive interface opens a zero-thickness interface governed by a fedoo.constitutivelaw.CohesiveLaw.

The IPC examples (ipc_* and the other scripts using IPCContact or IPCSelfContact) require the optional dependency ipctk (pip install fedoo[ipc]). Some of them also need gmsh to mesh curved geometries.
